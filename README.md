## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose Direct Farm Connect, an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers without unnecessary intermediaries.

The platform allows farmers to list their agricultural products, update available quantities, and set prices. Consumers and bulk buyers can directly browse products, compare prices, and place orders.

The platform provides:

Direct buying and selling between farmers and consumers.

Transparent pricing to ensure better earnings for farmers.

Bulk ordering facilities for retailers and businesses.

Logistics support for transportation and delivery.

AI-based demand forecasting to predict market requirements.

Route optimization to reduce transportation costs and delivery time.

Innovation and Uniqueness:

Our solution combines direct agricultural trading, AI-based price and demand insights, and smart logistics in a single platform. It aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries and create a transparent agricultural supply chain.

## Technical Approach
Technologies Used:

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap.

Backend: Python and Django.

Database: MySQL / SQLite.

AI and Machine Learning: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn.

Maps and Location: Google Maps API.

Development Tools: VS Code, GitHub.

Implementation Methodology:

Farmer registration and login.

Farmers upload products, quantities, and prices.

Consumers and bulk buyers browse available products.

Buyers place orders directly through the platform.

The system connects orders with logistics providers.

AI predicts demand based on previous sales and market data.

Route optimization identifies efficient delivery routes.

The platform tracks orders and delivery status.

Working Flow:

Farmer → Product Listing → Digital Marketplace → Consumer / Bulk Buyer → Order Placement → Logistics → Delivery.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:

The proposed system can be developed using affordable and widely available technologies such as Python, Django, HTML, CSS, and machine learning libraries.

A web-based platform can be accessed through smartphones, tablets, and computers.

Potential Challenges:

Limited digital awareness among farmers.

Transportation and logistics management.

Maintaining product quality during delivery.

Predicting agricultural demand accurately.

Building trust between farmers and buyers.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges:

Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.

Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.

Introduce product quality details and transparent order tracking.

Improve AI predictions using historical sales and demand data.

Provide secure registration and transparent payment records.
